## My Contribution — Amaka Mary Nduka

For this assignment, my team analyzed Pfizer's $43 billion acquisition of Seagen Inc. through an Enterprise Risk Management lens. My specific contributions covered three areas.

**Opportunities (SWOT Matrix)**
I identified the external opportunities this deal creates for Pfizer. The acquisition doubles their oncology pipeline to 60 programs and is projected to generate over $10B in revenue by 2030. I also highlighted how Seagen's ADC technology opens doors to new drug combinations, global market expansion, and fresh partnerships with biotech and academic institutions.

**Financial, Regulatory & Strategic KRIs**
I developed the key risk indicators for this category, focusing on metrics like debt service obligations on Pfizer's $31B financing, FDA submission timelines, IRA Medicare negotiation impacts, foreign exchange exposure, and ESG-related trends.

**Contributing Factors & Controls**
This was the most detailed part of my work. I mapped out eight risk categories — from integration execution and cybersecurity to people risk and compliance — linking each one to its root causes and the preliminary controls in place to manage them.
